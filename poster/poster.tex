\block{1\quad Why this matters}{
% SOURCE [ekberzade2025upinflames] READ_FULL: ~60,000 ha, four ignitions, 10 days to 2 weeks to contain
The 2021 Manavgat fire (Antalya) burned about \hl{60{,}000 ha}; it started at four separate points and took 10~days to two weeks to
contain~\cite{ekberzade2025upinflames}.
% SOURCE [tarimorman2025] (#13): 776 towers, 184 smart, 11 min (REVIEW: figures verified by the user)
T\"urkiye already watches its forests from 776 lookout towers (184 of them ``smart'') and has cut average first response to
% SOURCE [giglio2020modis] READ_FULL sec 8.3.2: ~1000 m2 routinely
\hl{11 min}~\cite{tarimorman2025}. MODIS routinely detects fires of about 1{,}000\,m\textsuperscript{2}, smaller ones only in very good
conditions, and only at overpass times~\cite{giglio2020modis}; camera towers claim smoke detection over tens of kilometres (vendor claims, Section~4).

\vspace{3mm}
\hl{The gap:} the first minutes, while the fire is still smouldering under the canopy. Ground and trunk sensors can sense those
minutes: gases, VOCs and pyrolytic particulates appear before visible smoke~\cite{fonollosa2018chemical,khan2022recent}.
Commercial deployments exist (Dryad Silvanet~\cite{dryad_silvanet}, N5 Sensors~\cite{n5sensors_chemshield}, ForestGuard~\cite{forestguard_nowildfire}),
and analytical models already link sensor density to detection time or burned area for randomly placed sensors~\cite{fierens2009number,pandey2018modeling}.
\hl{Our contribution} is one open, end-to-end model that joins radio range and multi-hop connectivity, sensing reach, energy and cost,
parametrised for \textit{Pinus brutia} forests at \pv{f.mhz}\,MHz, with every input labelled as measured, vendor or assumed.
}

\block{2\quad Research questions}{
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{RQ1}} & How close must a fire be before a low-cost node detects it (gas, VOC, PM, temperature)? \\[1.5pt]
\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{RQ2}} & What radio range do trunk-mounted and scattered nodes get under the canopy? \\[1.5pt]
\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{RQ3}} & How sparsely can nodes be scattered and still form one connected network? \\[1.5pt]
\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{RQ4}} & How soon is a fire detected, and how soon does the centre know? \\[1.5pt]
\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{RQ5}} & How long does a node live on a battery, and can a small solar cell make it perpetual? \\[1.5pt]
\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{RQ6}} & Which authentication or encryption scheme fits, and how long can keys stay in a node? \\[1.5pt]
\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{RQ7}} & Which point in cost $\times$ density $\times$ lifetime $\times$ delay is best for a given forest? \\
\end{tabularx}
}

\block{6\quad Results: range and connectivity}{
% SOURCE REGULATION [etsi300220 Table B.1 band M, btk_sinif1 row 20]: 25 mW e.r.p.
{\small\textbf{\textcolor{fire}{Model inputs (literature \& ITU standards).}} Radio at \pv{f.mhz}\,MHz, \pv{tx.dbm}\,dBm transmit power (inside the 25\,mW e.r.p.\ band limit~\cite{etsi300220,btk_sinif1}), SX1262 sensitivities~\cite{sx1262ds}.
% SOURCE [boonlom2026experimental] (READ_FULL via WebFetch; 923 MHz, gateway 8 m; 0 dBi = ASSUMPTION), [ferreira2020study] (READ_FULL pdf)
No \pv{f.mhz}\,MHz path-loss data for \textit{P.~brutia} were found, so each deployment is bracketed: trunk at \pv{trunk.h.m}\,m, $n=\pv{nexp.M.cons}$ (assumed);
scattered, $n=\pv{nexp.S.opt}$--$\pv{nexp.S.cons}$, $-\pv{detune.db}$\,dB detuning; $\sigma=\pv{sigma.opt.db}$\,dB (assumed) to \pv{sigma.cons.db}\,dB (forest measurements in~\cite{itur_p833}). A trunk value $n=\pv{nexp.M.opt}$
is Boonlom's fit for a 923\,MHz forest link to an 8\,m gateway; it reproduces their \pv{fe.d.m}\,m/\pv{bl.d.m}\,m losses by construction (\pv{bl.pl.250}/\pv{bl.pl.1200} vs \pv{up.pl.250}/\pv{up.pl.1200}\,dB~\cite{boonlom2026experimental})
but not a near-ground rainforest link (\pv{fe.pl.lo}--\pv{fe.pl.hi}\,dB at \pv{fe.d.m}\,m~\cite{ferreira2020study}): an \emph{unverified upper bound}.
% TODO-URL: repository link not yet public; do not invent one
Code to be released.\par}

\vspace{3mm}
% KEEP-LITERAL: 90 % link reliability = FADE_Q (1.28 sigma) definition
\hl{Range (RQ2).} At \pv{f.mhz}\,MHz the first Fresnel radius at mid-path of a \pv{fresnel.d.m}\,m link is \pv{fresnel.r1.m}\,m, so neither
a trunk mount nor a ground node clears it. 90\,\% link range at SF7 (SF12): trunk-mounted \textbf{\pv{range90.M.cons.sf7}\,m} (\pv{range90.M.cons.sf12}\,m), conservative;
scattered \textbf{\pv{range90.S.cons.sf7}--\pv{range90.S.opt.sf7}\,m} (\pv{range90.S.cons.sf12}--\pv{range90.S.opt.sf12}\,m), conservative--optimistic; upper bound
\pv{range90.M.opt.sf7}\,m (\pv{range90.M.opt.sf12}\,m).

\block{7\quad Results: detection time}{
\hl{Detection (RQ1, RQ4).} A point ignition grows as a disc at constant speed $v$ (no acceleration or spotting); young \textit{P.~brutia}
surface fires spread at \pv{ros.lo}--\pv{ros.hi}\,m/min~\cite{kucuk2012rates}, base \pv{ros.base}\,m/min. A node detects by heat within
% SOURCE [dampage2022forest] READ_FULL: 5 m node sensing range (multi-sensor node)
$r_T=\pv{rt.lo}$--$\pv{rt.hi}$\,m (assumed; 5\,m is the range taken in~\cite{dampage2022forest} for a multi-sensor node, method not reported), sampled every \pv{ts.heat.min}\,min, or, inside the
downwind sector of half-angle $\theta=\pv{theta.deg}^\circ$, by gas within $r_G=\pv{rg.0}$--$\pv{rg.2}$\,m (assumed), sampled every \pv{ts.gas.min}\,min plus the
\pv{gas.resp.s}\,s BME688 response~\cite{bme688ds}. For Poisson density $\lambda$, $\lambda I(T)$ nodes have detected by $T$:
\[
\begin{gathered}
% KEEP-LITERAL: formula constants
I(T)=\int_0^{\infty}\Big[\big(1-\tfrac{\theta}{\pi}\big)p_T+\tfrac{\theta}{\pi}\big(1-(1-p_T)(1-p_G)\big)\Big]\,2\pi r\,dr,\\
p_X(r)=\Big[\big(T-t_X-(r-r_X)^+/v\big)/T_{s,X}\Big]_0^1\ \text{(clipped to $[0,1]$)},
\end{gathered}
\]
% KEEP-LITERAL: ln 10 = ln(1/(1-0.9)) belongs to the 90 % target (params.DETECT_TARGET_P)
so $P(\text{detected by }T)=1-e^{-\lambda I(T)}$ and \pv{target.pct}\,\% needs $\lambda=\ln 10/I(T)$. $(r-r_X)^+/v$ is the front's time to reach range $r_X$, not gas travel; adding travel $\min(r,r_G)/U$, $U=\pv{adv.U.lo}$--\pv{adv.U.hi}\,m/s (assumed),
raises the $r_G=\pv{rg.1}$/\pv{rg.2}\,m densities by \pv{adv.rg50.U2.pct}--\pv{adv.rg50.U0.5.pct}/\pv{adv.rg100.U2.pct}--\pv{adv.rg100.U0.5.pct}\,\%. Fig.~1, Fig.~4 and the tables in Section~5 all use this expression.

\block{9\quad Security}{
\hl{Goal (RQ6).} Alarms are not secret: stop forged, replayed or suppressed messages (authenticity, freshness, availability).
Symmetric cryptography only.
\vspace{1mm}

{\small\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.05}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.27\linewidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X l@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Threat} & \textbf{Response} & \textbf{Status} \\
\midrule
Forged / replayed alarm & per-node key, \pv{tag.alarm}\,B tag, counter + replay window & mitigated$^\ast$ \\
Jamming, duty-cycle exhaustion & missing heartbeats flag a hole; relays cannot verify end-to-end tags, so junk is forwarded & not mitigated (relays) \\
Captured node & revoke its key; others unaffected only if no shared beacon or group key exists & partial \\
Opening, glitching, side channel & tamper switch detects opening, not removal; erase reach (flash vs backup registers) unverified & out of scope \\
Fake heat, masked sensor & cloud cross-checks neighbours (not modelled) & partial \\
Sinkhole / selective forwarding (routing) & hop-count adverts and forwarding are not authenticated; a captured relay can drop or delay alarms & not addressed (open) \\
Fake time beacons & beacon authentication key unspecified (open design point); nonces use counters, not time & open \\
GNSS spoofing (scattered) & position sent once, authenticated; a spoofed fix misplaces one node & partial \\
Gateway / cloud breach & standard IT security & out of scope \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}\par}
{\footnotesize $^\ast$assuming the keys stay secret.\par}
\vspace{1mm}
\begin{itemize}
% SOURCE [zhu2006leap]: individual key K_u = f_Km(u) (LEAP, CCS'03 version read; LEAP+ abstract read)
\item \textbf{Keys.} $K_u=\mathrm{KDF}(K_m,\mathrm{id}_u)$, $K_m$ off the nodes~\cite{zhu2006leap}; an authenticated, replay-protected \emph{key update / change}
command from the gateway confines a capture to one node (its own stored keys stay exposed). Keys are loaded at installation (trunk) or in the factory (scattered).
% SOURCE [stm32wle5ds] READ_FULL: SRAM retained in Stop2 (sec 3), flash endurance (Table 66), AES modes (sec 3.21)
\item \textbf{AEAD.} Hardware AES-\pv{key.bits}~\cite{stm32wle5ds} in CCM mode~\cite{nist80038c}; nonce counter\,(\pv{nonce.counter.B}\,B)\,$\|$\,epoch\,(\pv{nonce.epoch.B}\,B)\,$\|$\,node~ID\,(\pv{nonce.node_id.B}\,B),
\pv{nonce.B}\,B (CCM: 7--13\,B, unique per key). The receiver rebuilds the epoch from its low byte in the
\pv{hdr.B}\,B header. Counter in RAM (kept in Stop2~\cite{stm32wle5ds}), reset by a power loss; at boot the node writes epoch\,+\,1 to flash before its first frame
(\pv{flash.kcycles}\,k write cycles~\cite{stm32wle5ds}). Keys in protected flash survive a drained supercapacitor, and nonces should not repeat
if the epoch write completes; power-fail safety and wear levelling of this write are open.
Receivers track the highest counter per node and epoch and rate-limit failed tags.
% SOURCE model: TX = I_TX14 x V x ToA (macros tx.frame.mJ.*); AES = params AES_* ASSUMPTION #22 (cycles NOT FOUND); software Ascon energy: no source (RESEARCH R17)
Hardware AES-CCM is used instead of software Ascon-AEAD128 because the radio dominates frame energy (TX \pv{tx.frame.mJ.lo}--\pv{tx.frame.mJ.hi}\,mJ per SF7 frame,
\pv{tx.mA}\,mA\,$\times$\,\pv{vsup}\,V\,$\times$\,airtime; AES $\approx$\pv{aes.uJ}\,\textmu J, assumed \pv{aes.mA}\,mA, \pv{aes.cyc} cycles at \pv{aes.MHz}\,MHz, unverified;
software Ascon not measured) and hardware AES adds no airtime.
\item \textbf{Tag length.} An \pv{tag.alarm}\,B tag gives $2^{-\pv{forge.tag8.exp}}$ forgery chance per try; NIST's Ascon guidance tolerates up to
% SOURCE [nist2025sp80232] READ_FULL sec 4.3 R4-R5: 2^(lambda-32) failures for lambda >= 64
$2^{\lambda-32}$ failures only for $\lambda\ge64$-bit tags and asks to limit failures to one below that~\cite{nist2025sp80232}, so no \pv{tag.short}\,B tags.
\item \textbf{Physical, quantum.} Readout protection of related STM32 parts has been bypassed cheaply~\cite{obermaier2017shedding} (no side-channel or fault
claim); Grover's speed-up~\cite{grover1996fast} leaves AES-\pv{key.bits} about \pv{key.grover.bits} bits; NIST: AES-256 category~5, AES-128 category~1~\cite{nistir8547}.
\end{itemize}

\block{10\quad Limitations and roadmap}{
{\small
\begin{itemize}
% SOURCE [noerthemann2013early] READ_FULL sec 3: 0.5 m2 -> 25 m, 2 m2 -> 105 m (H2 sensors); [coskuner2026fire] 5 km/h
\item \textbf{Sensing:} $r_G$, $r_T$ assumed and fixed; gas sensing works from ignition with an $r_G$ that does not grow with fire area (optimistic), yet measured reach grew with fire size (0.5\,m\textsuperscript{2}: 25\,m; 2\,m\textsuperscript{2}: 105\,m, H\textsubscript{2}~\cite{noerthemann2013early});
gas travel only as a sensitivity (WP1).
\item \textbf{Radio, fire:} no \pv{f.mhz}\,MHz \textit{P.~brutia} path-loss data, gateway antenna = node antenna, no collisions or ACKs (WP2, WP3);
constant-speed fire without acceleration, spotting or crown fire; wind-driven runs of \pv{ros.wind.kmh}\,km/h ($\approx$\pv{ros.wind.mpm}\,m/min)~\cite{coskuner2026fire} are out of scope.
\item \textbf{Energy, false alarms, load:} canopy light, supercapacitor ageing and PM2.5 not measured or budgeted; in low light PV does not carry a gas
node and the supercapacitor never fills (WP4). False alarms and collisions are not modelled; at \pv{load.dens}/ha one channel carries \pv{load.pct}\,\% airtime
of hourly \pv{hb.B}\,B heartbeats over \pv{side.ha}\,ha. One-node alarms are assumed; a two-node confirmation needs $\lambda I=\pv{k2.x2}$ instead of \pv{k2.x1} ($\times$\pv{k2.ratio} density).
% KEEP-LITERAL: 2 = two nodes drifting in opposite directions (macros.py drift.ms = 2 * CLOCK_PPM * SYNC_PERIOD_S)
\item \textbf{Timing:} unbudgeted for listen slots, the relative drift of two nodes ($2\times\pv{clock.ppm}\,\text{ppm}\times\pv{sync.s}\,$s) is \pv{drift.ms}\,ms, beyond the \pv{pre.sym}-symbol
preamble (\pv{pre.ms}\,ms, SF7); sync period, preamble and guard need joint design.
\item \textbf{Security, position:} no side-channel or fault tests (WP5); ``fewest hops'' routing is unauthenticated (Section~9); position accuracy requirement, under-canopy fix rate and quality, and the
empty-supercapacitor start unmeasured.
\end{itemize}\par}
